Search a list from the add box: what you type narrows the list, ticked items included, with a button to clear it and one to add. The app opens on the list you used last, which you can turn off in Settings, and Settings has a Text size choice as well. A name typed on its own gets the amount it had last time, and adding something already ticked off brings it back rather than adding it again.

Remembered amounts need version 1.11 of the Shopping List server app.
