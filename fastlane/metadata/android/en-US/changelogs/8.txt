Use the app without an account, with your lists kept on the phone and uploaded later if you sign in. Join a list shared with you from its link or invite code, no Nextcloud account needed, and see who added and ticked each item. Add photos to items, and paste a whole list to add one item per line. Also a light theme, pinned lists, sorting for lists and items, and moving items between lists.

Some of this needs version 1.10 of the Shopping List server app.
